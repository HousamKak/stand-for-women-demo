THE MARION FUND

Keeping her memory.
Rebuilding futures.

The Marion Fund honors Marion Hochar Ibrahimchah, who lost her life in the Beirut port explosion on August 4, 2020.

Created to support women-owned businesses affected by the blast, the fund prioritizes vulnerable women, businesses that sustain jobs, and enterprises preserving Beirut’s heritage and culture.

Its support has included reconstruction, in-kind assistance, cash support and opportunities to develop business skills.
