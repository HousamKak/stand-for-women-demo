A SHARED COMMITMENT TO WOMEN

Our partnership with Wing Women Lebanon, Melie the founder keeps on fighting to end period poverty by producing reusable pads. She is creating job opportunities for women from vulnerable communities. The pads are all sold commercially or/and to other organizations (NGO/charities), since period poverty is affecting 76% of those menstruated in Lebanon. By this, we are providing economic empowerment to women whilst simultaneously reducing period poverty in Lebanon and being eco-friendly.

A STAND FOR WOMEN COLLABORATION

Beirut Marathon Women’S Race

We participated in the Beirut Marathon Women’s Race to support our cause. We had 315 women, men and children running for Stand for Women and contributed at helping women-owned businesses in Beirut. Participants were Individuals and 14 corporates and Initiatives; which were Holdal GAA, Arab Bank, CMACGM, L’Oreal Lebanon, Entotox, FNF, Grant Thornton, Tinol, Total energies, Vitas, Merit shipping, KFF Khalil Fattal, Bemo Bank, Cannelle and Life 4 youth. Moreover, our partners during the race were Bio Oil and Entotox.

Feel22

This year 2023, on Women’s Day March 8, 10% of the proceeds from Feel 22 sales was donated to our cause. And with this amount we were able to support 4 hard working women-owned small businesses located in Rmeil, aiming for their continuous growth!
