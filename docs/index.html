Enterprise
A STAND FOR WOMEN COLLABORATION

Al Fanar

Stand for Women is proud to have Alfanar Venture Philanthropy as a partner in the Marion Fund. Alfanar is historically a strong supporter of social enterprises. After the devastating Beirut explosion they have dedicated funds to alleviate small businesses & has pooled efforts with Stand For Women to restore some women-owned stores.

Leadership
A STAND FOR WOMEN COLLABORATION

FNF Lebanon

Our collaboration with Friedrich Naumann Foundation (FNF) Lebanon was to support 11 women-led SMEs providing them with digital marketing and e-commerce training. Thanks to Tomkeen , Stephan Bazan and Maria Frangieh , who facilitated the amazing workshop. In which, Participants got 23 hours of group training, 30 hours of tailored one on one sessions and 15 hours of customized workshops.

A STAND FOR WOMEN COLLABORATION

Beirut Footsteps × Elsa Voyage Voyage

In collaboration with Beirut footsteps and Elsa voyage, we promoted 8 Women-owned guesthouses from different areas in Lebanon on our social media platforms.

THE MARION FUND

From heartbreak,
a helping hand.

Created in memory of Marion Hochar Ibrahimchah after the Beirut port explosion, the Marion Fund supports women-led businesses as they rebuild their livelihoods.

170+SMEs supported
295SMEs assessed
