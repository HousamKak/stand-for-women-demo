OUR STORY

A shared purpose.
A stronger tomorrow.

We are Stand For Women. A Lebanon-based non-governmental organization championing women’s economic empowerment, gender equality and sisterhood since 2017.

Independent women.
Interconnected futures.
WHAT WE STAND FOR

Opportunity is
for every woman.

We envision an environment free of discrimination and violence, where women have equal opportunities to lead, work and thrive.

Our work connects women-led businesses with practical support, develops leadership and confidence, and introduces young people to inspiring women across industries.

From supporting vulnerable businesses through crises to creating connections between women, we put economic independence at the heart of lasting change.
